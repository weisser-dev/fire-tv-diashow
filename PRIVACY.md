# Privacy Policy — Photo Carousel

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Photo Carousel displays images that you choose. It does not operate an account service, upload local images to its developer, or include advertising or analytics.

## Images and device data

When you choose local images, Android grants the app read access to those files. The app stores the selected file references and playback preferences on the device. The image files remain in their original locations and are not copied or uploaded by the app.

When you use Google Fotos, the app requests the Google Photos Picker read-only scope. It creates a temporary Picker session, displays Google's Picker URL as a QR code, and downloads only the image items you explicitly select. Those selected images are cached in the app's private storage for slideshow playback. The app keeps the access token in memory for the active Picker session; it does not save the token to disk or send it to the developer.

Google receives the OAuth, Picker, and image-download requests needed to provide its service. Those requests are governed by Google's privacy policy and Google Photos API policies. The developer does not receive a copy of your photos.

## Retention and deletion

Selected image references and playback settings remain on this device until you clear the selection or uninstall the app. Google Photos images downloaded for playback remain in the app's private cache until you clear the selection or uninstall the app. Clearing the selection also releases Android's saved file permissions and removes the app's Google Photos cache.

## Children's privacy and changes

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children or any other user. If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the changed handling is released.
