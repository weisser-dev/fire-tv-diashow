Remote context menu · click to try
Portrait Fire TV displayMenus, remote navigation, and photos follow the chosen app orientation while the HDMI output stays in landscape.
Local photos and albumsBrowse, sort, preview, and remove photos on the TV. Add pictures from a phone over the same Wi-Fi.
Calendar, tasks, and notesShow the next events and local reminders on the display. Use the phone QR page to hide or restore recurring calendar titles.
Preloaded photo transitionsThe next image is ready underneath the current photo. The current image fades out without a black flash; motion on the new image starts afterwards.
Smart shuffleRandom playback favors new and less-seen photos. View counts are saved locally on the TV.
Sleep and scheduleShow a dim clock, calendar, and reminders during sleep hours. Set a sleep timer or a weekly playback schedule.
